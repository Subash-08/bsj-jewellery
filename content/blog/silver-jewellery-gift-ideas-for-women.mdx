The best jewellery gift feels like it belongs to the person receiving it. Before browsing, think about what she already wears: a simple chain every day, a statement ring for occasions, or a kolusu with a personal connection.

## For someone who loves simple pieces

A [silver chain](/silver-jewellery/chains) or [pendant](/silver-jewellery/pendants) can be a versatile starting point. Compare the listed chain length with something she already owns. If you are unsure of her taste, choose a design that fits her usual wardrobe.

## For someone who enjoys a personal detail

A [silver ring](/silver-jewellery/rings) can feel especially thoughtful, but size matters. Check an existing ring worn on the same finger or use our [size guide](/size-guide). A [bracelet](/silver-jewellery/bracelets) can be another option when you know her preferred fit.

## For a traditional touch

A [silver kolusu](/silver-jewellery/anklets) can celebrate a family tradition or a milestone. Review the anklet's length, clasp and product details before ordering. Our [kolusu buying guide](/blog/how-to-choose-silver-kolusu-size-design-purity) walks through those checks.

## Before you place the order

Check availability, the material and purity stated for that product, its measurements and the delivery estimate at checkout. If timing matters, read the [shipping policy](/shipping-policy) before purchase.
