Kolusu is more than an accessory for many families. It can mark a celebration, become a thoughtful gift or simply be a piece you enjoy wearing every day. When choosing [silver anklets](/silver-jewellery/anklets) online, the individual product details matter more than the category name.

## Measure for the fit you prefer

Use a flexible measuring tape around the part of your ankle where you want the kolusu to rest. Allow room for comfortable movement. Compare your measurement with the length listed on the product page; do not assume every design has the same fit. If a pair is listed, confirm whether the size refers to each anklet.

## Think about the design and daily routine

A simple chain style can be easy to pair with everyday clothes. More detailed designs may suit an occasion or a gift. Look closely at the clasp, links and any decorative elements in the product photos. Consider whether the style feels comfortable with your usual footwear.

## Read the stated purity and weight

Silver jewellery can have different fineness grades. The **Bureau of Indian Standards** explains that silver hallmarking provides third-party assurance of a marked article's fineness; a purity claim on a product listing is not, by itself, proof that every article is hallmarked. Read the details for the exact item and ask for clarification if its mark or documentation matters to you. See the [BIS hallmarking overview](https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/?lang=en) for the official explanation.

Weight can also affect how an anklet feels. Check the weight on the product page, especially when comparing a delicate chain with a fuller design.
