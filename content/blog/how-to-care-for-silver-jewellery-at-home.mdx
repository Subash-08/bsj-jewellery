Silver jewellery can change appearance as it reacts with its surroundings. A few careful habits can help your favourite pieces stay ready to wear. Always start with any care instructions given for the particular item, especially if it has stones, plating, enamel or an oxidised finish.

## Store pieces separately and dry

After wearing a piece, gently wipe it with a soft, clean cloth. Let it dry before storing it in a pouch or lined box. Keeping chains separate helps prevent tangles and scratches. The [Gemological Institute of America recommends](https://www.gia.edu/articles/gia-news-research-tips-caring-jewelry) anti-tarnish storage for sterling silver.

## Keep perfume and household products away

Put jewellery on after perfume, lotion and hair products have dried. Remove it before cleaning, swimming or other activities that expose it to chemicals. This is particularly useful for an everyday [silver ring](/silver-jewellery/rings) or [bracelet](/silver-jewellery/bracelets).

## Clean gently

For plain silver, a soft polishing cloth may be enough for light tarnish. Avoid rough brushes, abrasive powders and strong chemical dips unless the product's care instructions specifically allow them. If a piece includes stones or a special finish, ask a professional before trying a cleaning method.

## Check clasps and links

Before wearing a [silver chain](/silver-jewellery/chains) or [kolusu](/silver-jewellery/anklets), take a moment to check that its clasp closes securely. If a link feels loose or damaged, pause wearing it until it can be assessed.
